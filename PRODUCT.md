# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Teachers preparing lessons and exam revision, then teaching online or hybrid classes. They use the app as a teaching station, including alongside Google Meet.

## Product Purpose

Help teachers teach more easily by bringing lesson preparation, exam revision, and classroom workflows into one supportive station. Reduce the effort needed to prepare and run a class, while keeping teachers in control of their materials and meeting workflow.

## Brand Personality

Supportive, clear, and trustworthy. The interface should feel appropriate for education and help teachers stay confident and focused while teaching.

## Anti-references

No specific anti-references were provided. Keep the experience education-appropriate and avoid visual or interaction choices that make teaching tasks harder to understand or interrupt classroom flow.

## Design Principles

- Support lesson preparation and live teaching as equally important parts of the teacher's workflow.
- Make common teaching and exam-revision tasks easy to find and complete.
- Keep classroom, lesson, and meeting state clear so teachers can act with confidence.
- Integrate Google Meet into the workflow while keeping the teacher in control.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific accessibility standard or user-specific requirements were identified. Preserve platform accessibility semantics and affordances in future UI work.
